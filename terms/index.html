Non-custodial — you hold the keys

Your recovery phrase and private keys are generated on your own device and never transmitted to us in any form — see the Privacy Policy for the technical detail. A direct consequence of that design:

  • We cannot see, access, freeze, recover, or reverse your funds, your keys, or your recovery phrase, under any circumstances.
  • If you lose your recovery phrase and your device, your funds are permanently inaccessible — to you and to us. There is no password reset, no account recovery, and no support ticket that gets them back.
  • Any transaction you sign and broadcast is final once it's confirmed on-chain. We have no mechanism to cancel, reverse, or refund it, even in cases of fraud, a mistyped address, or a mistaken amount.
