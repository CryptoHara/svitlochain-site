Svitlo Wallet

Your non-custodial multi-chain wallet

Store, send, and receive SVIT alongside BTC, ETH, USDT, SOL, XRP and more — all in one wallet. Your keys are generated and encrypted on your own device — we never see them, store them, or can recover them for you.

Open Web Wallet → Download for Android (APK) iOS — Join the TestFlight beta

The web wallet runs entirely in your browser — nothing to install. Android install requires enabling "install from unknown sources" once, since this build isn't on the Play Store yet. iOS requires the free TestFlight app.

Read the privacy policy →

Web Wallet

  1. Open svitlochain.com/app in any modern browser (desktop or mobile).
  2. Choose Create new or Restore from phrase, and set a password.
  3. Back up your recovery phrase — it's shown once and never stored anywhere.

Optional: your browser's menu lets you "install" it as an app icon for one-tap access — no app store needed.

Android

  1. Tap Download for Android above to get the APK.
  2. Open the downloaded file and allow "install from unknown sources" when prompted (one-time).
  3. Launch Svitlo Wallet and create or restore your wallet.

iOS (TestFlight beta)

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store if you don't have it yet.
  2. Tap Join the TestFlight beta above and accept the invite.
  3. Install Svitlo Wallet from TestFlight, then create or restore your wallet.

Non-custodial

Your recovery phrase and private keys never leave your device. Your keystore is encrypted on-device (AES-256-GCM) before it ever touches disk.

Cross-platform

Built for the browser, iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, and Linux — the same wallet across every device you use.

Multi-chain

Send, receive, and stake SVIT, plus hold and move BTC, ETH, USDT, USDC, SOL, XRP, LTC, DOGE, ATOM, DOT, NEAR and more — connected to the Svitlo Chain network, or to your own node.
