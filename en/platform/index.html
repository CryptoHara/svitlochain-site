1. Install the client

Download the Svitlo Chain client for your OS and connect it to your GPU. Windows, macOS, and Linux will all be supported at launch.

2. Connect your wallet

Link a Svitlo Wallet address — the same non-custodial wallet already live today — to receive earnings or pay for compute.

3. Start earning or building

GPU owners set their price and availability; AI developers deploy jobs via CLI or API. Everything settles in SVIT on Svitlo Chain.

For AI developers

Deploy via CLI or API, specify your GPU requirements, and pay only for compute actually used — no long-term contracts, no minimum commitments.

For GPU owners

Connect idle hardware — gaming rigs, mining rigs, data centers — and earn passive income. You set your own price, availability window, and hardware specs.

Secured by Svitlo Chain

Every job is cryptographically verified and settled in SVIT on Svitlo Chain's own Layer-1 blockchain — no middlemen, no markups.
